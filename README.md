# 平行人生 · Parallel Life

<p align="center">
遇见那个"做了不同选择"的自己 · <em>Meet the version of yourself who took a different path.</em>
</p>

---

## 这是什么 · What is this

**平行人生**是一个让你能与"另一个自己"展开一场真实的对话——那个在某个路口做出了不同选择的你。

**Parallel Life** is a Skill that guides you through a structured life-reflection experience: explore a key decision point, meet a believable "parallel self" who took a different path, and have a real conversation with them.

---

## 能做什么 · What it does

- 🔍 **人生回溯**：通过轻柔的问题，梳理你生命中的重要分叉点  
  *Life retrospective — gently surfaces the key fork points in your life*

- 🪞 **平行自我生成**：基于真实因果逻辑，构建一个可信的"另一个你"（有遗憾，有收获，不是完美的）  
  *Parallel self generation — builds a credible alternate you, grounded in realistic cause-and-effect, with their own regrets*

- 💬 **角色扮演对话**：与平行自我展开真实对话，探索两条人生轨迹的交汇与分歧  
  *Role-play conversation — talk to your parallel self, compare paths, discover what you each envy in the other*

- 📄 **输出留档（可选）**：生成人生对比报告（Markdown）或星空主题交互网页（HTML）  
  *Optional output — generate a parallel life report or an interactive star-map webpage as a keepsake*

- 🌱 **回归当下**：每次体验结束时，通过着陆仪式帮你平稳回到现实  
  *Landing ritual — every session ends with a grounding exercise that brings you back to your actual life*

## 安全设计 · Safety Design

平行人生涉及真实的情感与记忆，安全始终优先：

*This Skill touches real emotions and memories. Safety comes first:*

- **随时可以离开**：每个阶段都有自然的退出节点，以关心口吻提出，不是系统弹窗  
  *Exit at any time — each phase has a natural off-ramp, offered as genuine care, not a system prompt*

- **不做价值判断**：平行自我永远不会说哪条路"更好"或"更差"  
  *No value judgments — the parallel self never declares any path "better" or "worse"*

- **情绪监测**：全程监测情绪信号，出现波动时自动启动安全协议  
  *Emotional monitoring — safety protocol activates automatically if distress signals are detected*

- **平行自我不是治疗师**：TA是另一个你，是同伴，不是心理咨询  
  *Parallel self is not a therapist — they're a peer, not a counselor*
